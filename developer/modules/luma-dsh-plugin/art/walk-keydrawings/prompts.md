# 行走重绘提示

以下五套由内置 ImageGen 绘制，每次使用该造型自己的招呼图与鲸鱼娘原装招呼图。第一张决定身份和服装，第二张提供游戏内的头身比例参照。

Use case: identity-preserve. Production 2D anime desktop game walking character sprite animation. Reference 1 is the authoritative model sheet for exactly ONE character, outfit, skin tone, original shoes, silhouette, hair and face. Keep the same sweet Q version and visible head size from that greeting. Reference 2, blue maid, supplies only the game's head-to-body and ink/paint finish, do not borrow any of her colors or costume. FOUR by FOUR regular grid of exactly sixteen separate full body drawings on truly transparent background. Every cell equally sized, one single sprite per cell, comparable scale, no overlap. Row 1 she walks forward FACE ON, facing camera. Row 2 she walks LEFT, nose and toes point to viewer's LEFT. Row 3 she walks AWAY, back of head to camera, only back of her costume visible, no facial features. Row 4 faces RIGHT in true profile, nose and toes point viewer's RIGHT. In each row column 1 left shoe steps onto ground in front, right foot trails, opposite arms swinging. Column 2 right knee passing, lift right heel. Column 3 right shoe steps in front, left foot trails, arms exchange. Column 4 left knee passing with raised left heel. Visible motion, readable shoes, controlled short steps equal to one shoe length, planted foot. Keep hips and head stable, round giant head, abbreviated round torso, small hands, stubby legs, toy proportions 2.1 heads tall (anatomical top of head excluding accessories to chin is about HALF the body), original adorable cheeks and eye size, minimal thin soles. Detailed illustrated colors and folds, gentle cel-shading. Slight follow-through of locks and skirt while head angle is stable. Fit accessories in each cell and generous transparent gutter. Beside the character the entire canvas is empty transparency. No ground, shadows, aura, labels, gridlines, square back plates, no camera perspective changes. Very crisp dark colored outlines, natural face at 120px on screen.
