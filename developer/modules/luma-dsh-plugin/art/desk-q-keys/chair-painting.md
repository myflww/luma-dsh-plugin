# 坐姿与实椅联合原画

内置 ImageGen 使用本项目的原始房间椅子和角色招呼图作为参照。接待台从来访者一侧取景，卧室依原房间的椅面方向取景。

## 卧室联合原画

Paint the CHARACTER AND THEIR EXACT CHAIR TOGETHER in ONE composition, as a production sprite. Reference 1: the exact empty room chair and camera angle — copy the form, angle, perspective, color, seams, legs/casters and upholstery; reference 2: exact chibi character, outfit and head-to-body proportions. Produce a transparent 2x2 sheet with FOUR identical-perspective full chair+character assemblies. DO NOT draw a desk, screen, table, keyboard, floor or any background, shadow blob, lines, writing, sheet grid. View from same rear-oblique side as the provided chair, character facing AWAY to the upper right, looking at imaginary monitor; the large upholstered back at left comes IN FRONT of her lower torso. The character sits deep enough to compress the seat, torso leans slightly forward from her pelvis, knees pass the lip and lower legs and small shoes hang FREELY off the FRONT end (right side of image), below cushion height. Seat and thighs foreshorten along an identical perspective axis. At side, nearest armrest blocks the chair-side hip and part of lower arm, while the visible near forearm passes over it and reaches out beyond it to type. The near and far hands sit at slightly different depths on imaginary desk keyboard. Their toes are below the cushion, legs short and proportions cute. Model overlaps, hips and bent knees IN THE SAME DRAWING as the actual chair. Crisp illustrative character features matched with the soft lit chair finish. Character's real hair should drape around, behind and in front of appropriate places on chair. LOCK complete chair placement, width, height, legs and rigid geometry identical between four cells. Head width is about .62 of chair width, chibi head and shoulders rise above the backrest naturally while torso is nestled into the seat. Four subtle exposures: near hand pressing, near wrist lifts and far hand reaches, far fingers press, wrists settle. Fixed hips and feet. All four show the seated working pose, 1 character+chair per cell, clean gutter, full chair feet within cell, fully transparent exterior. No separate unoccupied chair, no levitation or hovering, no ghost figures.

## 前台联合原画

Make a game-ready JOINT DRAWING of an identical Q chibi office receptionist and the EXACT chair she occupies. Reference 1 is the authentic character and costume. Reference 2 is the actual world's camera and BLACK MESH RECEPTION CHAIR. Isolate the chair in reference 2 as your furniture model and direction: front view, almost perfectly centered, no side profile, slightly high camera looking down 20–25°, small rectangular black mesh curved backrest, dark charcoal padded side armrests, swiveling black base. No counter, desk, keyboard, equipment, floor, wall or any piece of the reference room may appear! Paint a transparent 2x2 FOUR-frame sprite source. Each of FOUR cutouts is the same complete seated character AND same complete chair, including casters, grounded and in one coherent perspective. Both of her eyes and shoulders visible. The character occupies the chair naturally. The black backrest behind her torso is narrow and mostly hidden. Seat visibly supports pelvis, her thighs come forward toward viewer comfortably, short shins over lip and little shoes point toward floor. The left and right armrests are about 1.18 helmet/head widths apart, either side of her waist and partially cover the lateral costume but not her hands. Arms come down from shoulders with bent elbows, forearms reach forward as if to type, wrists extend toward the viewer in front of torso slightly below rib cage. Her gaze is lower than straight ahead, toward an unseen terminal between her and viewer. Four subtly different typing poses: TOP LEFT left fingers press, right wrist lifts; TOP RIGHT wrists pass one another's typing heights; BOTTOM LEFT right fingers press and left lifts; BOTTOM RIGHT wrists return. Keep chair structure rigid and same proportions, pelvis, head, feet all registered. Match ink and painted cel shading of reference 1, large rounded chibi head, original clothes, eyes/visor/ears/halo/accessories, plump short limbs. Stable front view with modest perspective foreshortening. Each cell shows one whole character-and-chair with no separate empty chair. Fully transparent outside the combined silhouette, no scenery/shadows behind castors, no design text, no labels, no cell lines, no grid border. Consistent generous gutters, never crop crown, hair or wheels.

## 其他卧室联合稿的动作约束

 Reference 3 is a previous demonstration for this exact chair in the game: use ONLY its scale, projection, support and occlusion. REPLACE its occupant entirely with the model from Reference 2. Never borrow the example occupant's hair color, headgear, shoes or outfit. The simple chair remains practically pixel-registered across all four beats; make the subtle finger alternation clear. Keep character identifiably two-head chibi, rear 3/4 oriented facing the desk to upper right, with visible near forearm and two distinct dangling little feet.

## 斜侧面工位

从角色背后约 55 度、倾角约 25 度观察坐姿。四格共用镜头、臀部支点和双脚落点，依次画近手落键、近腕抬起、远手落键和双手回位。头身、衣装、发量及鞋形取自角色自己的招呼原画。源图在 `high-oblique/`，以工位的原椅和键盘决定最终坐深与左右朝向。

三把椅子的原场景参照保存在 `reference-chairs/`。两间卧室的联合稿在 `joint/`，前台的联合稿在 `reception/`。房间光照及近侧遮挡以实际场景画面为准。
