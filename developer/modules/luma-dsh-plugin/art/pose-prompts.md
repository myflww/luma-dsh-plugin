# 角色动作原画

内置 ImageGen 以每套造型自己的 Q 版招呼图为角色参照。以下是成套生成时的共用提示，单张构图与动作以提交的透明原画为准。

## 抓起、悬空与落地

Identity-preserving high quality Q chibi animation model sheet, transparent PNG. Match the provided reference character's identity, costume, ears/horns, accessories, hair volume/color, shoes, tail if present, lineweight and beautiful painterly finish exactly. Exactly FOUR SEPARATE full-body game sprites in a spacious 2x2 grid, no overlaps. Identical camera scale, standing proportions about 2.15 heads high, very short round legs, stable big head and cheek outline. TOP LEFT: just lifted gently by an invisible grip between the shoulders, hands throw upwards in surprise with elbows bent, feet curl up asymmetrically, small surprised mouth. TOP RIGHT: still suspended by the same invisible shoulder grip, hands settle near chest or reach for balance, one knee bent up, other leg dangles, hair and skirt trail due to gravity, expression easing. BOTTOM LEFT: landing, BOTH little shoes touch ground, knees compress deep with hips lower, arms out for balance, hair tips still bouncing up and bright relieved smile. BOTTOM RIGHT: rear three-quarter turned toward UPPER RIGHT, seated on invisible chair, compact thighs project forward, short shins below seat at right angle, hands forward typing at an invisible desk. First three face viewer. Significant articulated acting in shoulders, elbows, hands, knees, toes, natural follow-through. No duplicated limbs, no cheek patches or graphic symbols, no furniture, scenery, background or shadows; leave transparency and margins around each figure, all shoes and tips inside their own cells.

## 卧室坐姿

Identity-preserving illustrated game sprite atlas, matching the supplied reference character EXACTLY, retain Q-version super-deformed 2.15-heads-tall proportions, huge rounded head, short plump thighs and shins, same hair, costume and color details, line weight. One sheet of FOUR complete figures, 2x2 equal grid, transparent empty background, no furniture, no text, no shadows. All four show the SAME small character sitting on an INVISIBLE CHAIR, gently typing on an invisible keyboard on an invisible desk. Their pelvis stays on the SAME seat line across each row, knees bent around a right angle, short calves below seat, little matching shoes. Both forearms and palms extend FORWARD in the direction of gaze. Adopt moderate isometric top-down camera (about 20 degrees above), as if photographing a small doll from behind. TOP LEFT: true straight rear view, see back of head, back, skirt folds, arms reach forward to invisible keys, start typing. TOP RIGHT: exact same rear view of the exact SAME scale and silhouette with other hand/fingers gently pressing keys. BOTTOM LEFT: REAR three quarters rotated 35 degrees toward UPPER RIGHT of picture, back clearly visible, only tiny sliver of far cheek acceptable, facing away from camera, bent knees project forward/right, one cuff and hand visible. BOTTOM RIGHT: exact same rear 3/4 angle position and height with alternating wrist/fingers quietly typing. No portrait, front or profile face, no head turn, no long slender legs, no stretching. Ensure full hair, tail if present, both shoes within each cell, with generous gaps between sprites. Keep any halo, hair loops, bows or accessories attached to the correct outfit. Exact costume identity. This is new game art, not a diagram; no gridlines.

## 四向行走

每套新画位于 `art/walk-keydrawings/<角色>-<造型>.png`。每张透明原画有四行四列。行从上到下是面向镜头、向左、背向镜头和向右，列从左到右是左脚接地、右膝经过、右脚接地和左膝经过。图集构建会保留这四个实际画面，把触地的鞋对齐到第 258 像素，并沿用原角色的完整步行周期。可以用 [四向改前改后对照](../../../../docs/images/walk-proportions.gif) 逐套查看。

陆弥与吉米的 ImageGen 输入分别是自己的 `art/actor-poses-keys/<角色>-<造型>.png` 及 `jingyuniang-base.png`。前者确定角色和衣服，后者确定脸与身体的比例。三套共用的生成提示如下。每次生成在第一段后加入对应角色的身份描述。

> Illustrate a finished game production walk-cycle sprite sheet for the SINGLE character and EXACT costume in reference image 1. Reference 2 (whale maid) is ONLY the standard for proportions and line finish. Never include the maid, blue hair or maid costume.
>
> Draw EXACTLY 16 FULL-BODY artworks in a uniform 4 columns × 4 rows orthographic turnaround grid. Rows (strict): 1 front facing camera/down, 2 pure profile walking toward viewer’s LEFT (nose points left in every cell), 3 back walking AWAY from camera (back of hair no face), 4 pure profile walking toward viewer’s RIGHT (nose points right in every cell). Columns within each row: four consecutive distinct grounded bodily walking key cels: left shoe stepping forward heel contact, weight passing over left foot right knee crosses, right shoe stepping forward heel contact, weight passing over right foot left knee crosses. Gentle alternating arm swing; read differently on adjacent frames. Same relaxed, friendly expression, side hand separation, articulate feet. Both legs SHORT and visible under the skirt, shin and thighs compact, never lanky: enormous round head 50% silhouette height excluding accessory, 1.95–2.05 heads tall from crown to shoes, with forehead and face round as the reference maid; consistent head size across all rows. Steady walking on flat ground: support shoe meets equal invisible floor line in each cell, tiny step amplitude, no levitation or splayed legs. Hair flows only a touch. Delicate fine colored line and detailed soft anime shading matched to reference 1; high-quality cute Q-version collectible proportions. Equal width/height cells, generous transparent padding around every separate isolated character, their halos/horns and two shoes fully inside own cell. Transparent RGBA, no background, floor, shadows, props, outlines around cells, labels, letters, white edging or watermarks. Professional readable high-resolution sprite atlas, stable design and scale.
